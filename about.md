I'm Arkadeep, reading for a BSc in Statistics at Presidency University, Kolkata.

Most of what goes up here falls into one of three buckets:

- **Proofs I want to keep.** Arguments that struck me as clean, or ones I had to fight with before they clicked.
- **Coursework worked out properly.** Probability, distribution theory, linear algebra, inference — written the way I wish the textbook had written it.
- **Problems.** Olympiad-flavoured combinatorics and probability, and the occasional quant interview question.

The house style is: state the assumptions, say why each step is allowed, and don't hide the load-bearing lemma.
